# Local fonts

- `Manrope-Variable.ttf`: full Manrope 200–800 from the official Google Fonts repository https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope, downloaded 2026-10-27; `manrope-OFL.txt`. Used for body text, controls, product titles, prices and administration; includes ₹.
- `DMSerifDisplay-Regular.ttf`: full regular 400 from https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmserifdisplay, downloaded 2026-10-27; `dmserifdisplay-OFL.txt`. Used for editorial, page and panel headings and the existing Muvira wordmark, without synthesized bold.

Both fonts are unmodified, locally served under SIL Open Font License 1.1 with `font-display: swap` and preloaded by the entry document. Serif headings always use regular 400; product titles retain Manrope and administration keeps its existing weights.
